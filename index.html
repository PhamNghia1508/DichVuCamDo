The problem

Every group chat has this thread.

Five people, five tastes, and the plan goes to whoever gives up last. Ask a chatbot and it averages the loudest opinions into somewhere nobody’s excited about.

TasteCircle reads each person’s taste through Qloo’s cultural graph, scores every option for every friend, and won’t sign off on a night that leaves someone out.

How it works

One taste graph, an agent that checks its own work, and no averaging people out.

  1. i.

    Read every taste

    Favourites resolve to Qloo entities with /search — artists, films, TV, books, podcasts, places. Taste analysis on /v2/insights fingerprints each friend; /v2/insights/compare finds the bridge between the two furthest apart.

  2. ii.

    Score for every friend

    /v2/insights runs once for the group and once per friend, with venue-category tags and explainability. A harmonic-mean consensus means one unhappy friend sinks a pick.

  3. iii.

    Decide, critique, refine

    A tool-calling agent picks the night, runs bridge searches when someone’s left out, and a critic rejects drafts that ignore them. Ask for a change and it goes back to Qloo — never to its imagination.
